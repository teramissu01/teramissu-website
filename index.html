    11FAQ
    
          
    Good
    questions.
    
          
    Something else on your mind? Email info@teramissu.com — we reply within one business day.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            Can our data stay in Australia on AWS?
            
    Yes. AWS runs two Australian regions — Sydney (ap-southeast-2) and Melbourne (ap-southeast-4). We set guardrails that keep workloads and data in Australian regions where required, and use the second region for in-country disaster recovery.
    
          
    
          
    
            Do you help with the Essential Eight?
            
    Yes. We baseline your maturity against the ASD Essential Eight, then implement AWS controls, automation and evidence collection to reach your target level — and stay there.
    
          
    
          
    
            Can we use generative AI without sending data overseas?
            
    Often, yes. Amazon Bedrock is available in Australian regions, and we design AI with data residency, access controls, logging and evaluation aligned to the Privacy Act and Australian Privacy Principles.
    
          
    
          
    
            We're a small business — is this for us?
            
    Definitely. Plenty of engagements start small: a health check, a cost review or a two-week AI proof-of-concept. We'll suggest the smallest step that delivers real value.
    
          
    
          
    
            How does pricing work?
            
    After a free 30-minute discovery call we send a written proposal in AUD (plus GST) with scope, deliverables and timing. Choose fixed-scope, time-and-materials or embedded engineering.
    
          
    
          
    
            Do you work outside Melbourne?
            
    Yes. We're on-site across Melbourne and regional Victoria and work remotely with clients in Sydney, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Canberra, Hobart and Darwin — travelling for key workshops when it helps.
